# T1.2 — Patient and sample table

Status: complete with explicitly missing clinical fields. Supplementary Table S3.

`tables/samples.parquet` and its TSV view retain all 46 clinical biopsies from
23 patients. The clinical tables agree on every shared field. There are eight
nivolumab patients, nine pembrolizumab patients and six combination patients.
The immune object covers 45 biopsies and 22 patients: `sc5rCMA188` from GC39
(on-treatment only) is absent. This is consistent with a reported biopsy
exclusion, but the exact exclusion cause is not independently established.

The tables identify 20 patients with clinical pre/on sampling. GC16 has two
baseline and three on-treatment biopsies. Every possible pairing is retained
in `tables/candidate_pairs.tsv` and flagged; none was arbitrarily selected.

Rows include patient, sample, timepoint, treatment, tissue, anatomical site,
chemistry, preservation, object-cell totals and total author-annotated CD8 cells.
Clinical response is explicitly named `response_for_stratification_only`.
Total TME counts are unavailable without the priority-two object and remain
missing. Zero cells for the absent sample means zero cells **available in this
object**, not biological absence of immune cells.

Biopsy dates, interval bounds and lesion IDs are unavailable. Site-text agreement
does not establish a lesion match. These fields remain missing; no values are
imputed. Both the missing interval and ambiguous pairing constraints are carried
into the provisional eligibility output.

The independent original-RDS count check agrees with every exported-biopsy CD8
total. Evidence: `reports/evidence/clinical-source-reconciliation.json`,
`cohort-audit.json`, `cohort-counts-r-validation.json` and the sample/pair tables.
Hashes are in `reports/EVIDENCE_SHA256.json`.
