# Feasibility stop before lockbox assignment

Status: a necessary sample-size condition for G1 fails. This is a count-only
precheck, not a completed T1.7 assessment of state availability or predictability.

The primary anti-PD-1 monotherapy cohort cannot support the current design using
the deposited author CD8 annotations. No state proportions or changes were
examined to reach this conclusion.

| Minimum CD8 cells in each biopsy | Maximum monotherapy patients passing cell counts | Development patients after holding out four | G1 requirement met? |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 30 (primary) | 3 | Not allocatable | No |
| 15 (pre-specified sensitivity) | 6 | 2 at most | No |
| 50 (pre-specified sensitivity) | 3 | Not allocatable | No |

These are **upper bounds**, not final eligible-patient counts. Patient-specific
intervals are unavailable, lesion identity is unverified, and multiple biopsies
need a pre-specified rule. The three count-eligible primary patients are GC15,
GC22 and GC36. The threshold is applied to total CD8, not separately to each
state. Counting directly from the original RDS independently reproduces the
neutral-export totals for every biopsy.

G1 requires at least ten development patients. Even omitting a lockbox would
leave only six count-eligible patients at the lower pre-specified threshold.
Reducing state granularity does not increase the number of CD8 cells sampled.
Combination patients remain separate, as required by the plan.

Additional limitations:

- Both clinical Table S1 workbooks and the immune metadata lack biopsy dates or
  elapsed-day bounds. The plan's expectation of obtaining individual intervals
  from Table S1 is not supported by these files.
- Four lockbox patients would also be insufficient for a one-sided exact
  Wilcoxon rejection at alpha 0.05 (minimum p = 0.0625).
- The deposited object differs from the published cell count and retains author
  doublet/cycling labels. It is not evidence that all QC and lineage ambiguities
  have already been resolved.

## Consequence for execution

T1.3 remains provisional. T1.4 is not run: no `LOCKBOX.json` is created.
T1.5, the complete G1 report, reference fitting and model development remain
blocked. The spatial runtime and Jev stages are not started.

The next useful decision is a protocol revision. The preferred direction is to
retain SPECIAL as a pilot/descriptive cohort and find or add a cohort with enough
paired CD8 observations for the primary prediction study. An alternative is a
strictly exploratory SPECIAL analysis with explicitly reduced claims and revised
validation; it cannot be presented as satisfying this plan's G1. A limited
lineage audit could check whether the full TME object contains additional usable
CD8 cells, but recovery must be demonstrated rather than assumed.
